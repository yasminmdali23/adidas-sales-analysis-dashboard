# Adidas Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

A corporate-level Power BI sales operations dashboard analyzing retail revenue, operational profitability, and market penetration for Adidas product ecosystems. This dashboard helps stakeholders track sales targets, regional performance, and top-tier retail distributor channels.

## 📊 Key Metrics & Insights
* **Total Revenue Generated:** $900 Million
* **Operating Profit:** $332 Million
* **Operating Profit Margin:** 42%
* **Total Units Sold:** 2 Million units
* **Average Price Per Unit:** $45

## 📈 Features & Visualizations
* **Temporal Trend Tracking:** A line chart evaluating monthly sales fluctuations, highlighting seasonal spikes in July ($95M) and August ($92M).
* **Geospatial US Sales Map:** Interactive map of the United States detailing regional sales concentrations and market penetration.
* **Regional & Product Segmentation:** Donut chart evaluating regional performance shares (West region leading at $270M / 30%) alongside product line performance (led by Men's Street Footwear at $209M).
* **Distributor Analysis:** A ranked bar chart showcasing performance metrics across retail giants, identifying West Gear ($243M) and Foot Locker ($220M) as premier distribution partners.
* **Date & Region Filters:** Dropdown slicers filtering the report instantly across exact invoice dates and sales territories.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI Desktop:** Power Query Data Cleaning, KPI Cards, Shape Map Visuals, and Interactive Slicers.

## 📂 How to Use
1. Download the `.pbix` file.
2. Launch **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the top filters to select specific date ranges or geographic sales regions.
